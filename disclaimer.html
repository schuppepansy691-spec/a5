Vision Science Disclosures & Optical Disclaimer

Effective Date: January 1, 2026•PupilAcademy Academic Governance

1. Educational and Scientific Research Purpose

The treatises, mathematical formulas, wavefront aberrometry models, and pupillometry analyses provided on PupilAcademy are formulated solely for educational, academic research, and engineering reference. They are not intended as individual medical advice, surgical recommendations, or direct clinical prescriptions.

2. Independence from Commercial Diagnostic Instrument Manufacturers

References in our Optics Journal to specific wavefront sensors, aberrometers, corneal topographers, or pupillometers (such as Hartmann-Shack, Scheimpflug, Placido, or infrared video trackers) are made strictly for scientific and comparative educational purposes. PupilAcademy operates as an independent vision science academy.

3. Non-Clinical Educational Status

Individuals experiencing acute vision changes, ocular pain, or sudden pupillary asymmetry (anisocoria) should immediately consult a licensed medical optometrist, ophthalmologist, or emergency neuro-ophthalmologist for comprehensive clinical evaluation.

4. Mathematical Modeling and Optical Variances

All calculations presented in our interactive Aperture Workbench reflect standard paraxial and non-paraxial mathematical models. Physical biological variation in human corneal asphericity, tear film stability, and crystalline lens gradient refractive index may yield different empirical measurements in vivo.
