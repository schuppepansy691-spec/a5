Privacy Policy & Research Data Governance

Effective Date: January 1, 2026•Vision Science Privacy Standards

1. Commitment to Academic Discretion and Digital Privacy

At PupilAcademy Vision Institute, we hold the privacy, personal discretion, and intellectual confidentiality of our optical scholars, research fellows, and digital visitors in the highest regard. We operate under strict data minimization principles, adhering fully to the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and international ethical standards for academic data stewardship.

2. Scope and Nature of Information Collected

To process fellowship applications, coordinate wavefront aberrometry masterclasses, and distribute optics research publications, we collect essential professional details (such as full name, institutional affiliation, email address, telephone number, and research scope notes). Technical browsing telemetry, including pseudonymized IP addresses and device browser parameters, is gathered exclusively to maintain secure encrypted connections and optimize platform rendering performance.

3. Lawful Basis and Purpose of Processing

Personal data is processed strictly to respond to academic consultations, manage fellowship enrollments, dispatch scientific monographs, and provide technical guidance on vision optics. We never utilize member data for automated behavioral profiling, intrusive tracking networks, or unsolicited commercial marketing campaigns.

4. Absolute Guarantee Against Commercial Data Sale

PupilAcademy enforces an absolute institutional guarantee: we never sell, lease, monetize, or publicly disclose academic records or contact lists to third-party marketing syndicates, ad networks, or data brokers. Information is shared solely with essential operational infrastructure providers—such as secure cloud servers and encrypted academic email delivery systems—under strict non-disclosure obligations.

5. Cryptographic Security Architecture and Data Retention

All digital communications and inquiry transmissions are protected using modern TLS 1.3 encryption protocols during transit and AES-256 military-grade encryption at rest. Scholar records are maintained securely within isolated databases, accessible solely by authorized academy administrators.

6. Universal User Privacy Rights and Contact

Scholars maintain full legal rights to review, update, export, or request the immediate erasure of their personal records at any time. To exercise your privacy rights or discuss our data stewardship protocols, please contact our Data Governance Officer at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, or telephone our concierge desk at +1-888-777-5845.
